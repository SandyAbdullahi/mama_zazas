About Mama Zaza

Handcrafted cookies with a Nairobi soul. From the aroma of the oven to the surprise in each flavor—this is where nostalgia meets indulgence.

Born in Nairobi

Mama Zaza began in a tiny kitchen in Ngara, with handwritten recipes passed down through generations. Our cookies aren’t just sweets—they’re moments. Each batch is mixed by hand, baked in small quantities, and infused with local love.

Whether you're grabbing a weekly flavor or sending a dozen to your cousin in Kileleshwa, you’re part of a story that started with one oven and a whole lot of heart.

Our Cookie Philosophy

We believe in rotating weekly flavors so no cookie lover gets bored. We balance nostalgia with bold twists—think Red Velvet meets Coconut Chai or Lemon Crinkle with Nairobi Vanilla.

Locally Sourced Small Batch Weekly Rotations Crafted with Joy

Meet the Bakers

Mama Zaza herself still crafts the base doughs—and our baking crew tests every new flavor before launch. Each cookie is baked in-house, cooled to perfection, and wrapped with our signature pink paper twine.

No machines. No shortcuts. Just good old-fashioned cookie love.

From our mixer to your mouth—the journey is short, sweet, and unforgettable.
