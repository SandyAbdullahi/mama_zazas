About Mama Zaza

Handcrafted cookies with a Nairobi soul. From the aroma of the oven to the surprise in each flavor—this is where nostalgia meets indulgence.

From a Humble Home Kitchen

Mama Zaza’s journey began with a single oven in her Nairobi home—a warm space filled with the aroma of butter, brown sugar, and big dreams. Driven by the desire to share rich, homemade baked delights, she spent late nights perfecting recipes that comfort and connect.

Each cookie still honors those early days: baked in small batches, wrapped with love, and served as more than just a treat—they're a taste of home from a woman with heart in every bite.

Our Cookie Philosophy

We believe in rotating weekly flavors so no cookie lover gets bored. We balance nostalgia with bold twists—think Red Velvet meets Coconut Chai or Lemon Crinkle with Nairobi Vanilla.

Born from a Mother's Heart

Mama Zaza began in her own kitchen—where love, warmth, and a sprinkle of tradition blended into every batch. With her hands and heart, she crafted cookies that felt like home.

Today, every dough still carries that same spirit. No shortcuts. Just handmade goodness, inspired by a mother's care and baked to bring joy.
